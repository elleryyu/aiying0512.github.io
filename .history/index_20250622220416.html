Welcome

Welcome to the NeuroBioInformatics Lab (NBIL) of School of Data Science at the University of Virginia. Our research is in the field of mental health data science, focusing on statistical and computational approaches leveraging multi-modal imaging and genetics to understand typical and atypical brain development.

Graphical Models

Directed & Undirected modeling for network analysis

Machine Learning in Neuroscience

Statistical and deep learning methods for neural data analysis

Multimodal Fusion

Combining MRI, DTI, fMRI, MEG, and genetics data

Data Types

MRI, DTI, fMRI, MEG, SNPs, DNA methylation

Psychiatric Diseases

Schizophrenia, Alzheimer’s disease, Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder, etc.
