NeuroBioInformatics Lab (NBIL)

Advancing mental health data science: developing statistical and computational approaches leveraging multi-modal imaging and genetics to understand typical and atypical brain development.

Research Topics

Our research is in the field of mental health data science, focusing on statistical and computational methods that integrate multi-modal imaging and genetics to explore brain development and psychiatric disorders.

Graphical Models

Directed & Undirected modeling for network analysis

Machine Learning in Neuroscience

Statistical and deep learning methods for neural data analysis

Multimodal Fusion

Combining MRI, DTI, fMRI, MEG, and genetics data

Data Types

MRI, DTI, fMRI, MEG, SNPs, DNA methylation

Psychiatric Diseases

Schizophrenia, Alzheimer’s disease, Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder, ADHD, etc.
